Charging in Unexpected Places

Grand Rivers, KY · Day 169

Great in theory, underdeveloped in practice, I encountered several off-grid solar-powered EV chargers along the Zig-Zag. With a limited battery and out of repair motors for optimizing solar output, this concept didn't quite scratch the free-charging itch, yet I'm glad it exists.

The Mighty Bison

Theodore Roosevelt National Park, ND · Day 234

I didn't realize how large the range of the buffalo was until I began this journey. To see just how much of the States used to be plains and just how much the buffalo dominated was beautiful and somber. We have devastated their population almost to the point of collapse and are just now beginning to repopulate them. What powerful, impactful creatures and symbols of American expansionsim.

Read the entry →

Angel's Landing

Zion National Park, UT · Day 345

The natural beauty of the United States of America is unrivaled, especially with our capacity to build supporting infrastructure to see these quiet places. I was lucky to visit Zion National Park, one of our national treasures, while it was practically empty. This allowed me to hike Angel's Landing, a jagged cliff-face which gives a perfect vantage out over the valley.

Experiencing Culture

Saint John, NB · Day 84

The stories told today aren't mine to retell, but the experience of engaging with the culture of the First Nations and American Indians gave a depth of perspective beyond expectation. Take some time to explore the cutlure of the original landowners, there is history everywhere if you look for it.

The Purpose

To experience everything the United States has to offer. To learn the stories of as many people as possible, from rural communities to the biggest cities. To see the natural beauty the U.S. holds, from the mountains to the seas.

The End Goal

Use all of this knowledge to impact the conversation on comprehensive, nation-wide climate change resilience.

Meet the Man Behind the Zig-Zag

Benjamin Katz is a digital marketing generalist and strategic communicator with a love of the environment. He has always wanted to road trip across the nation and see what the North American continent has to offer. He plans on using this trip to learn more about how to communicate with people from all walks of life so that when the trip is over, he'll be able to accomplish his personal goal of improving sustainability-focused communications and empower more individuals to action.
