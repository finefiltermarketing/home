The Great American Zig-Zag

The mission? To drive from Maryland to Washington, visiting every contiguous state in a Great, American Zig-Zag, all in an electric vehicle.

Explore the Archive

The Purpose

To experience everything the United States has to offer. To learn the stories of as many people as possible, from rural communities to the biggest cities. To see the natural beauty the U.S. holds, from the mountains to the seas.

The End Goal

Use all of this knowledge to impact the conversation on comprehensive, nation-wide climate change resilience.

Meet the Man Behind the Zig-Zag

Benjamin Katz is a digital marketing generalist and strategic communicator with a love of the environment. He has always wanted to road trip across the nation and see what the North American continent has to offer. He plans on using this trip to learn more about how to communicate with people from all walks of life so that when the trip is over, he'll be able to accomplish his personal goal of improving sustainability-focused communications and empower more individuals to action.
